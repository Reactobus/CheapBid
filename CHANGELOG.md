# v2.1.0

First public release, for TBC Classic Anniversary 2.5.6.

- Full auction house scan with bid, buyout and time-left filters.
- Manual bidding and buyout from a sortable table, with price protection.
- "my bids" view that highlights outbid lots and lets you re-bid them.
- "Auc min" lowest-buyout price in the table and on item tooltips.
- "dis. loot" view of lots worth buying out and disenchanting.
- Optional modules: Disenchant, Prospect and CompactBrowse.
